## Status

Canvas is **in development**. `@texuli/canvas` is on npm as `0.17.5`. Under 0.x a new minor may break. The Kotlin twin validates canvas nodes and does not paint them yet.

## Not built

- A hosted realtime collaboration service (adapters are local or in memory)
- Persistence, network transport and image hosting (the host supplies them)
- Native painting of canvases
