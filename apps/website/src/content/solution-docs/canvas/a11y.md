## Keyboard and tree

The surface is the only `role="application"` region, named by the canvas title. Shapes take a roving tab stop in reading order. The shape tree (`role="tree"`) lists every shape and connector for screen readers. Changes are announced through the shared live region.

## Motion and contrast

Zoom and fit play through `texuli/motion` and are instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Forced colors keep outlines, focus and selection visible. Text paints as SVG text nodes; nothing is parsed as markup.
