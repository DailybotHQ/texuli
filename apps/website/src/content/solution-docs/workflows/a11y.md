## Keyboard first

On the canvas, arrows move focus to the nearest node in that direction, Alt+Arrow nudges one grid step, Space toggles selection, Enter on a port starts connect mode (arrows cycle compatible ports, Enter connects, Escape cancels), Delete removes, and Ctrl or Cmd with Z / Y undoes and redoes. `=` / `-` zoom, and `0` fits the view.

The outline is a WAI-ARIA treegrid that reads and edits the whole graph without the canvas. The palette adds a node with a button press as well as a drag (WCAG 2.5.7).

## Drag contract

The shared drag contract applies: localized announcements, 24 px targets, reduced motion and forced colors. Nothing renders as HTML.
