## Status

Documents is **in development**. `@texuli/document`, `@texuli/pages` and `@texuli/editor` are on npm as `0.14.4`. Under 0.x a new minor may break. Native rendering is planned, not built.

## Not built

- Paged-view editing (the paged view is read-only)
- Painted sheet and canvas embeds (they are typed references)
- A hosted editor, storage or collaboration service (the host supplies transport)
- Custom-format copy and drop in the rich-text editor, and a block menu of handles beyond the sortable handle
