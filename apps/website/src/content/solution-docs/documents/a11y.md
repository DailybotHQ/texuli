## Keyboard

The slash menu is an APG combobox. Block handles use Space or Enter to pick up, arrows to move, Space or Enter to drop and Escape to cancel; Alt+Up and Alt+Down move at once. Column splitters step with arrow keys. Every move is announced politely.

Text rows hand the caret between blocks at their edges. Enter splits; Backspace at the start joins.

## Motion and links

Inserts, removes and reorders use the shared motion runtime; `prefers-reduced-motion` and `motion={false}` are instant. Links pass `safeHref` and images `safeImageSrc` at the untrusted tier. There is no HTML sink.
